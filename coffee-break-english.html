Coffee Break English

Practice the English you hear every day

Pick a conversation

🎯 Speaking drills: Alex asks a question, you answer, Alex corrects you, and you say the better version again.
Your conversation partner is Alex (you can change this in ⚙️ Settings). Speak with the 🎤 button or type. After each thing you say, you'll see a corrected version and how a native speaker would say it.

🗣️ How do I say…?

Type or say what you want to say, in your own language or in broken English. You'll get the natural English, the key words, pronunciation tips and a short example dialogue, and then you can practise saying it.

🧩 Say it better

Type a rough idea: broken English, your own language, or a mix. You'll get it organized into clear spoken English, plus the words and linking phrases you need.

🔍 What did they mean?

Write down something a coworker said that you didn't get, even half of it. You'll get the meaning, the slang or cultural references, the tone, and good ways to reply.

Word bank

Words and phrases you save during practice show up here. Review a few every day; that's how they stick.

📈 My progress

These numbers update by themselves every time you practise. "Answers that were already right" counts the answers that needed no correction.

Week by week

📝 AI progress report

About once a week, tap the button. The AI reads your recent answers and corrections, estimates your speaking level, and tells you what improved and what to work on. Your teacher then practises those points with you in every conversation.

🧠 What Alex remembers about you

After your conversations, Alex writes short notes about you and reads them before every new conversation. You can change or delete anything here. The notes are saved only in this browser, on this device, and are sent to the AI along with your conversations.

Backup

Your phone and computer each keep their own memory. To move it, make a backup file on one device and restore it on the other. Backups include your memory, past conversations and word bank, but never your API keys.

Delete Alex's memory and all past conversations on this device? Your word bank stays.

☁️ Cloud sync

Keep your memory, past conversations, word bank and teacher settings the same on your phone and computer. Your learning data is saved in a secret Gist in your GitHub account, for free. A secret Gist is not listed anywhere, but anyone who has its exact link can open it, so API keys are never saved there. On a new device, paste your API keys in ⚙️ Settings yourself.

How to get a GitHub token (once, on a computer)
  1. Sign in at github.com, click your photo (top right) → Settings.
  2. At the bottom of the left menu: Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Tokens (classic).
  3. Generate new token → Generate new token (classic). Note: coffee english. Expiration: No expiration. Tick only gist.
  4. Click Generate token and copy it (it starts with ghp_). Paste it below on every device. The whole family can use the same token; each person uses a different sync name.

👋 One-time setup (2 minutes)

  1. Open Google AI Studio → API keys and sign in with a Google account.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it. It's free, and no credit card is needed.
  3. Paste it below and click Save & test.

Your key stays in this browser only. On Google's free tier, Google may use what you send to improve its products, so don't type private or sensitive information.

Already using the app on another device with cloud sync? Go to 🧠 Memory → ☁️ Cloud sync, enter your GitHub token and sync name, and your learning data comes back. (Your API keys are not synced; paste them here.)

Tips

• Use Chrome or Edge on a computer, Chrome on Android, or Safari on iPhone.
• On your phone, open the browser menu and tap Add to Home Screen so it opens like an app.
• If the 🎤 is blocked, click the icon at the left of the address bar and allow the microphone.
• If you hit a "free limit" message, wait a minute; the free tier limits requests per minute and per day.
